## Configuration
| Name | Default Value | Description
|:-----:|:-----:|:-----:
| `EasingDirection` | `In` | The [`EasingDirection`](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/EasingDirection) that will be applied to the movement of the teleported part.
| `EasingStyle` | `Linear` | The [`EasingStyle`](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/EasingStyle) that will be applied to the movement of the teleported part.
| `Instant` | `true` | Determines whether the part will be instantly teleported to the destination.
| `KeepVelocity` | `true` | Determines whether the part will keep it's intital velocity from before the teleport.
| `Offset` | `0, 3, 0` | Determines the offset in local space relative to the destination where the teleported part will be placed.
| `TweenTime` | `1` | The amount of time it takes for the teleported part to reach the destination position.
